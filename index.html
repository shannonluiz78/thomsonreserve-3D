Prime District 20 Reimagined

Unrivaled Scale & Proven Pedigree

Jointly developed by UOL Group, Singapore Land Group, and CapitaLand Development. Thomson Reserve offers two curated collections: The Classic Collection (four 21-storey towers) and The Luxury Collection (two 30-storey signature towers overlooking MacRitchie Reservoir).

Greenery & Nature

Perched beside MacRitchie Reservoir, Lower Peirce, and Bishan-AMK Park. Expansive unblocked green corridors with clean, open airflow and lush landscape coverage.

Transit & Amenities

A 6-minute sheltered stroll to Upper Thomson MRT (TE8) and Bright Hill Interchange (TE7/CR13). Directly opposite Thomson Plaza with dining enclaves along Upper Thomson Road.

Elite Education Belt

Within the highly coveted 1 km home-school distance to Ai Tong School. In close proximity to Catholic High, CHIJ St. Nicholas Girls', Raffles Institution, and Eunoia JC.
