About Thomson Reserve

A connected address in Upper Thomson.

Thomson Reserve is a 99-year leasehold development at Bright Hill Drive in District 20, near Upper Thomson MRT. Its 1,268 planned homes span two- to five-bedroom layouts across six residential blocks, with landscaped grounds and three clubhouses.

The developer is Tamarind Development Pte Ltd, a joint venture of CapitaLand Development, UOL Group and Singapore Land Group.

From direction to an estimated view

What would I look towards?

Routes to a more realistic future view

  • Licensed 3D surroundings: stream Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles in a separate compliant viewer, or obtain permitted OneMap3D access. Position the camera at a verified balcony height and bearing. Coverage, geometry age and occlusions limit realism.
  • Actual elevated photographs: ask the developer or an authorised capture operator for location- and height-tagged panoramas. These provide the strongest visual reference where lawful access and image rights are available.
  • AI impressions: useful for mood using licensed or owned photographs. A single ground image cannot reveal hidden roofs, terrain or distant obstructions. Do not label an invented elevated view as the future view.

Changing Street View pitch changes where the camera looks, not the height at which the photograph was captured. No real view imagery, paid API or AI image service is connected here.

1 · Locate the balcony

Confirm the stack’s balcony edge, compass bearing, floor elevation and ground level from the developer’s drawings. The camera brief currently contains estimates.

2 · Add real surroundings

Ask Huttons or SLA about permitted OneMap3D access for floor-level views. Licensed building heights and terrain can also support a reconstructed view. The 2D map alone cannot produce this image.

3 · Capture and label

Render each approved camera as an estimated reconstruction, or use a licensed photo with its capture location, height, direction and date. A street-level photo is not a high-floor balcony view.
